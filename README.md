# Michael P. Owens: Technical Deep-Dives

Architecture notes and business theses on the systems behind my ventures, written for a technical reader.

I direct AI coding agents under a written verification doctrine, set the product direction, and make the business decisions. These notes are the depth behind the one-line summaries on [michaelpowens.com](https://www.michaelpowens.com): the reasoning behind each system, how I evaluate the work, and what's demonstrated versus still open.

They deliberately stop at the IP boundary. No product source, no calibration constants, no thresholds, no corpus. Each note says what stays private. The point is the *shape* of the system and the reasoning, not the machinery.

- **[Strainwave](strainwave.md)**: building toward the force layer of the physical world. The radar direction, deployment economics, business thesis, and verification discipline. In R&D; the first market remains open.
- **[WarrantyBrain](warrantybrain.md)**: safe, idempotent claim automation, and a privacy-preserving cross-merchant fraud network. Live on the Shopify App Store.
- **[Fluxor](fluxor.md)**: multi-sensor fusion and a quality-gated pipeline that turns skilled physical work into robot-learning training data.
- **[BarbellAI — historical](barbellai.md)**: preserved notes on the earlier no-cameras sensing stack (inertial + mmWave radar), sim-to-real form perception, and validation discipline. Strainwave is the current force-measurement venture.

Two habits run through the work: **first principles before frameworks** (find the binding constraint, not the loudest problem) and **nothing is done until it's verified against the actual system**, where a second set of eyes with fresh context tries to refute every change before it ships. I record failures as verdicts; the honest FAILs are in here too.
